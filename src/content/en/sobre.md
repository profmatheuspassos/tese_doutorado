# About the dissertation

This site presents, in plain language, a doctoral dissertation in Law defended at the University
of Lisbon in 2022. The work compares Portugal and Brazil, two representative democracies: the
people elect those who decide on their behalf. The question is whether citizens can gain real
weight in public decisions without replacing this system with another.

<!-- B1 | O que o site é e o que não é | tradução de pt-br/sobre.md, B1; última frase: texto do autor (D103) -->

## What this site is, and what it isn't

The site is a version for the general public. The pages rewrite the argument in everyday words,
without changing its meaning. It is not the dissertation: the quotations, the notes, and the
detailed discussion with other authors are left out. To study or cite the work, use the official
version, in the [University of Lisbon repository](https://hdl.handle.net/10451/57093).

The site's texts were adapted into plain language with the help of artificial intelligence and
reviewed by the author. The connection between each passage and the part of the dissertation it
comes from is documented in the
[project repository](https://github.com/profmatheuspassos/tese_doutorado). The illustrations were
also generated with artificial intelligence, based on the author's instructions, and reviewed by
him. This English version was translated from the Portuguese with the help of artificial
intelligence and reviewed by the author.

## How the research was done

The author first read the text of the constitutions, laws, and court decisions, and formed his own
view. Only then did he compare it with what other authors had written, including those who
think differently. The comparison between the two countries is the basis of the work. Portugal
serves as a reference, because its laws are more open to citizen participation. The goal is to seek
solutions for Brazil. The research combines Law, Political Science, and Sociology, and also uses
numbers: opinion polls, election results, and official data on participation.

<!-- B4 | O que fica para depois | tradução de pt-br/sobre.md, B4 -->

## What is left for later

The work ends with six topics for future research. Among them: measuring whether the quality of
democracy improves, and studying ways to make parties accountable to voters. Another is studying
how to use technology as a means of participation without falling into direct democracy, where the
people decide everything without representatives.

## About the numbers

The numbers on this site are those of the dissertation, always with the year they refer to. They
have not been updated. They show the situation in the period studied, not today's.

Always cite the official version. The reference is below in three formats.

**ABNT**

```text

Tese (Doutorado em Direito, especialidade de Ciências Jurídico-Políticas) – Faculdade de Direito, Universidade de Lisboa, Lisboa, 2022. Disponível em: https://hdl.handle.net/10451/57093. Acesso em: [data de acesso].
```

